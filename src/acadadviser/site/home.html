What to expect

Labels are judgments

Reach, target and safety are the AI's view of your relative chances. They are not probabilities or promises.

Verify before you apply

Requirements and deadlines change, and AI can make mistakes. Always confirm on the official program page.

Community data is labelled

Admitted-student GPA medians are self-reported by applicants. They are not official cutoffs.
